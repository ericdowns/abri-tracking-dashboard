Glossary & lead types
Your lead types, in plain terms
Qualified lead: ready to talk. They booked a meeting, called the office, or used the contact form to ask you to reach out. These are your warmest leads.
General lead: just looking. They signed up for the newsletter or downloaded a guide. Worth nurturing, but not ready to talk yet.
Intent signal: on their way. They clicked to book or tapped your phone number, but have not finished yet. A sign a qualified lead may be coming.
New visitors. People landing on your site for the first time, rather than returning.
Schedule-call clicks. How many times visitors clicked to open your Calendly booking page.
Phone-number clicks. How many times visitors tapped your phone number to start a call.
Bounce rate. The share of visitors who arrive on a page and leave without visiting another.
Time on page. How long, on average, visitors spend on a page before moving on.
Year-over-year. This year's numbers next to the same period last year, so you can see real growth.
A/B testing. Showing two versions of a page to see which one gets more people to act.
G&M Capture. Our own popup and form tool that replaces the site's current popups, capturing signups and form fills as clean data.
G&M site tracking. Our lightweight tracking built into your site that records visits, clicks, and time on page without slowing things down.
Google Analytics. Google's website statistics tool, used here for your past history and comparisons.
Calendly. The scheduling tool your visitors use to book meetings.
CallRail. A phone-tracking tool that records calls coming from the website.
Leads by source. A breakdown of where your leads came from, such as Google Ads, Meta, organic search, or direct visits.
