Your website's real numbers, in one place you trust

Your site is doing real work, but the numbers that prove it are scattered across tools you do not control, and the tracking those tools pile on is what is slowing your pages down. This is a plan to fix both: one dashboard, built right into your own site, that shows the numbers that matter to you in plain language, without the weight.

What you would be able to see

  • How many new people visit, by day, week, and month
  • Meetings booked, phone calls, and newsletter signups, all in one place
  • Where your best leads actually come from, including your ads
  • Which pages hold attention the longest
  • A clear split between people ready to talk and people just getting to know you

How we would build it

We would build this in two phases, so you see it working before committing to the whole thing.

Phase 1

The foundation

The dashboard, live, running on the information we capture right on your site. This also clears out the unused tracking that is slowing your pages down, so a faster site comes built in. Nothing outside your site is needed for this part.

Phase 2

The connected pieces

Once Phase 1 is proving itself, we connect your scheduling and call tools to confirm exact bookings and calls, and pull in your past records so you can compare against last year. We scope this part after you have seen Phase 1 in action.

What we would need from you

Phase 1 needs nothing on your end beyond a green light. For the Phase 2 pieces, we would need access to a few of your existing tools: your scheduling tool, your call tracking, and your past website statistics. Some of that may live with your current marketing team, so part of the work is simply gathering those logins.

One thing worth saying plainly

The numbers here will not line up exactly with Google's or your ad platforms' numbers. Every tool counts a little differently, and that is normal. We will always be clear about which number to trust for what, so you are never left guessing.
